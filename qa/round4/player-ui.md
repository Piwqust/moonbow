# Round 4 - Player UI bug hunt

Build: build-r4. Instances: r4-player-*. Modes: windowed 1280x780 / 800x500 / maximized, fullscreen 2560x1440, PiP, mini player; dark and light. All input posted (no real mouse).

## Top 10

1. **P-08 (Major)** Side panel paints over the fullscreen video at a stale rect, with video bleeding around it.
2. **P-09 (Major)** Maximized -> fullscreen -> exit leaves a "zoomed" window at 0,0 2560x1440 (client 2544x1424) with frame borders, and the caption buttons are off the corner.
3. **P-04 (Major)** Tooltips stick after the pointer moves to the video. Cause pinned: ui\Host.cpp:411 ShowPopup -> HideTooltip nulls tooltipFor_.
4. **P-16 (Major)** The skip-chapter chip covers the subtitles in small windows.
5. **P-01 / P-02 (owner bugs 1 and 3)** confirmed: a see-through strip at the side-panel edge, and the window resizes on every file change (800x500 -> 760x1183).
6. **P-10 (Minor)** Going to the library also resizes and moves the window (800x500 -> 1280x780 at a new position).
7. **P-05 (Minor)** Control-bar tooltips are drawn on top of the bar's own buttons (no flip-up).
8. **P-11 (Minor)** Ctrl+Shift+P is ignored while a popover is open.
9. **P-12 (Minor)** No context menu in PiP.
10. **P-17 (Minor)** The quick-settings footer takes a third of the popover in short windows and cuts rows in half.

Owner bug 2 (queue not editable in fullscreen) did **not** reproduce with posted input (P-03). It probably needs a real cursor (auto-hide or cursor hide). Owner bug 4 is not re-tested (the source already has a floating bar in progress).
Note: source line numbers come from src\ at review time, and the fixer is editing PlayerView.cpp right now.

### Checked and working (posted input)
- Queue: right-click menu, Esc closes the menu and then the popover, drag-reorder upward (windowed and fullscreen), the popover survives F and re-anchors.
- A click outside a popover closes it and does **not** pause; Space works right after (focus returns).
- Fullscreen auto-hide: hides after ~2 s idle over the video, stays while hovering the bar, stays during a seek-bar drag, stays while a popover is open.
- Double-click toggles fullscreen without leaving a stray pause (single-click pause undone).
- PiP enters and leaves cleanly (480x270 bottom-right). The overlay buttons show on hover.
- Mini player: hover overlay and its queue popover work. The chapters tab updates when the file changes with the popover open.
- Wheel over volume changes volume, wheel over the seek bar seeks 5 s.
