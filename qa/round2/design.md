## Top 5

1. **N01 — Narrow-window header breaks.** At 800 px the page title vanishes and the "⋯" button is clipped by 19 px. It is the most visible layout failure, and it hits every library page.
2. **D23 — Two "primary" button styles** (white in empty states, grey in dialogs). In light theme the dialog primary and secondary are nearly identical.
3. **N03 / D03 — Overlays drawn straight onto artwork** (mini-player hover transport, now-playing glyph). They merge with the cover content and turn black on video in light theme.
4. **D07 / D24 / D16 — Menus.** Mixed filled and outline icons, checks on the left in popovers but on the right in menus, `#222222` instead of `bg.panel`, and no "Сведения о файле" in the file and player context menus.
5. **N04 + N05 + D29 — Settings polish.** English ON/OFF captions in the Russian UI, hard-clipped scroll edges, and disabled text still below 3:1 with no hint naming the parent toggle.

Overall: 16 of 32 round-1 findings are Fixed, 11 are Partly/Mostly fixed, 4 are Still broken (D07, D23, D31, and D03 in a new form), 1 could not be re-checked (D32), and none Regressed. 16 new findings: 1 Major, 5 Minor, 10 Polish.
