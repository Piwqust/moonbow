## Compared with the leaders

- **Thin progress line, 2× hold, skip intro, next-episode card:** at the level of uosc / ModernZ and Plex / Infuse in layout. Lumen trails only in emphasis: Netflix and Plex draw "Skip intro" as a high-contrast primary (R3-12).
- **Statistics:** carries the same data as mpv `stats.lua`. mpv's overlay has a near-solid backing and consistent number formatting, which Lumen's translucent panel lacks (R3-01).
- **Album header:** close to MusicBee / foobar2000 (art, year, genre, length, track numbers). The artist page is behind Apple Music / Spotify, which show album cards (R3-14).
- **Lyrics:** the Apple Music / Spotify layout (art left, synced lines right), without their edge masks (R3-15).
- **Type-to-filter:** works like uosc, but the result panel and empty state are less refined (R3-04).
- **Settings:** feature depth now matches MPC-BE / PotPlayer (upscalers, RTX, passthrough codecs, subtitle style). The presentation lacks value readouts and a subtitle preview, which those players and the YouTube / Netflix caption editors have (R3-07, R3-08).

## Top 5

1. **R3-01:** The statistics overlay is translucent with 2.7:1 labels and English decimals in the Russian UI.
2. **R3-02:** At 800 px, the "Add folder" tile fills the first screen of Videos and hides the actual folders (N10 and N11 are also still open).
3. **R3-06 + D29:** Disabled settings look wrong. Disabled toggles are brighter than enabled ones, and disabled labels are about 2.2:1 in light.
4. **R3-07 + R3-08:** Sliders without values, and a subtitle-style section without a preview.
5. **R3-03 + R3-04:** The corrupt-data toast covers the header with jargon. Filtered menus keep their full height and show "—" when nothing matches.

Overall: of the round-2 items re-checked, N01 and N04 are Fixed, N12 is Partly fixed, N10, N11 and D29 are Still broken, and nothing Regressed outright (D29 contrast is slightly worse). The new surfaces work. 16 new findings: 2 Major, 6 Minor, 8 Polish.
